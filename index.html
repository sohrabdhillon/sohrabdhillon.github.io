Design & analysis · Fall 2025

Compared stiffness, weight, material properties, and manufacturability of beam concepts. Developed a corrugated balsa–CFRP design and evaluated bending, shear transfer, deformation, and connector tolerances.

Simulation lead · Winter 2026

Evaluated four concepts and selected the balsa–CFRP design for its higher simulated stiffness than the XPS alternative. Projected assembly material cost: $3.74 against a $7.00 budget.

Developed a paired, four-beam arrangement with ONYX slot-and-rod connectors. Analysis predicted lower displacement and localized strain than the initial configuration. Refined laser-cut interfaces for repeatable alignment and tool-free assembly.

Stiffness and displacement comparisons are simulation results; costs are projected.
