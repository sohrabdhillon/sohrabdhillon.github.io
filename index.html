Design & simulation +

Compared stiffness, weight, material properties, and manufacturability across multiple concepts, selecting a corrugated balsa–CFRP design over the XPS alternative based on simulated stiffness and cost.

Developed a paired four-beam arrangement with ONYX slot-and-rod connectors. Bending calculations, second-moment-of-area analysis, and FEA were used to refine coupling geometry and reduce predicted displacement and localized strain.

Laser-cut geometry and connector interfaces were refined for repeatable alignment and tool-free assembly.
